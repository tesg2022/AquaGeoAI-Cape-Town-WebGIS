The solution

From complex geospatial data to clearer decisions.

AquaGeoAI Cape Town is being developed as a GeoAI-powered WebGIS that brings environmental data and spatial analysis into one practical decision-support experience.

The platform integrates Earth observation, climate data, geospatial analytics, and hydrological modelling to strengthen water security and climate resilience in Cape Town, South Africa.

It is designed to help stakeholders assess drought risk, analyse groundwater recharge potential, monitor appropriate water-quality indicators, and identify priority areas for climate adaptation.

By transforming complex geospatial datasets into interactive maps, predictive insights, and decision-support outputs, AquaGeoAI aims to help users identify vulnerable areas, prioritize interventions, and make more informed water-resource management decisions.

Platform capabilities are being developed and validated. Results should be interpreted with documented data limitations and model uncertainty.
